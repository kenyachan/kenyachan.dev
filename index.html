Robotic process automation

UiPath · University of Technology Sydney

Staff were keying the same data into the student system for thousands of requests a year. I built software robots in UiPath that process these transactions instead, freeing the team for work that needs a person.

Manual handling time the robots took over, per year (estimated from volumes)
Automation Volume a year Time each by hand Hours saved a year
Leave of absence applications 1,000–2,000 10 min 170–330
Digital transcript purchases 1,500–2,000 3–5 min 75–165
Alumni discounts 3,000–4,000 30 sec 25–35
Total 270–530

I also built a robot that applies scholarships to student records. Before building, I mapped each process in BPMN 2.0 with the teams who ran it.

Roster data extraction add-in

JavaScript and TypeScript · Office Add-ins (Excel JavaScript API) · University of Technology Sydney

The Student Enquiry Team kept its roster in a visual Excel workbook that was hard to analyse. I first updated an inherited VBA tool, then rebuilt it as an Excel add-in that turns the whole roster into a clean table the team can report on.

About 4 hours a week saved on roster reporting. I then adapted the add-in for a second team, the Future Learners Contact Centre.
